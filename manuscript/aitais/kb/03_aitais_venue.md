### Recommended structure (2026) [S3 4.2]

Introduction and problem statement → Analysis of related research → Methods or proposed approach → Experimental part or description of the study → Results → Discussion → Conclusions → Declaration on the use of generative AI ("which must comply with the requirements of the CEUR-WS template") → References ("an appropriate number of relevant sources sufficient to substantiate the research and consistent with its content and scope").

The 2025 guidelines were stricter and are useful as a reviewer-expectation baseline (no longer stated in 2026) [S18]:

- Section lengths: Introduction ≤1 p.; Related Work 1–3 pp.; Methods 2+ pp.; Experiment 2+ pp.; Results 2+ pp.; Discussion 1+ p.; Conclusions ≤1 p.
- Title of at most 13 words.
- At least 10 references, 70% from the last 3–5 years, self-citation ≤10%.

### Other formatting rules (2026) [S3 §4]

- Title: clear, matching the content, formatted per the template; "Manual line breaks should not be used in the title." (4.4).
- Figures: appropriate quality, informative captions, and "a textual description suitable for screen readers" (alt text) (4.6).
- Abbreviations spelled out at first use; formulas in template styles (4.7).
- "Articles must not contain page numbers. Footers, except on the first page, must be empty" (4.8).

## 8. CEUR-WS author-facing rules (AITAIS publishes there)

- **Template:** CEURART is required.
  - LaTeX (preferred by CEUR) or the ODT (LibreOffice). "Do not use Word for the ODT template." [S26]
  - "We require that the Libertinus font family is used in CEURART." [S26]; installation instructions are in the ODT [S12].
  - Modifying the template, "including but not limited to: adjusting margins, typeface sizes, line spacing, paragraph and list definitions — is not allowed" [S12].
  - Use the predefined styles Heading 1–3. No empty lines before headings. Table captions go above tables, figure captions below. Figures need screen-reader descriptions [S12].
- **Length classes:**
  - Regular paper: ≥10 "standard" pages (1 standard page = 2,500 characters, about 380–400 words).
  - Short paper: 5–9 standard pages.
  - Fewer than 5 pages counts as a poster/abstract and is not citable [S26].
  - AITAIS accepts regular papers only [S1].
- **Declaration on Generative AI:** a mandatory section placed above the References. It lists every GenAI tool and its contribution using the CEUR taxonomy (e.g., "Grammar and spelling check", "Paraphrase and reword") [S27, S28].
  - Mandatory for authors from 1 June 2025 [S27].
  - **Unacceptable:** GenAI-generated new text such as paragraphs or sections; GenAI-made diagrams, charts or illustrations (unless the paper is about automatic image generation) [S27].
  - Violations lead to removal of the paper or the whole volume [S27].
  - Template wording when no tool was used: "The author(s) have not employed any Generative AI tools." [S12]. The AITAIS guidelines give the variant "The author(s) did not use any Generative AI tools in the preparation of this work." [S3 5.2].
- **Title capitalization:** one style per volume (emphasizing capitalized style or regular English). Ask the editors [S26, S12]. Vol-4160 used regular English (sentence) case [S20].
  - No line breaks in the title, left-aligned [S12].
- **Authors:** use full first names wherever possible, and e-mails whenever possible [S12].
  - Corresponding author marked ∗; equal contribution marked † [S12].
  - Only natural persons can be authors [S26].
  - Non-Latin names must be transliterated; reuse the existing DBLP transliteration where there is one [S26].
- **ORCID:** the template has an ORCID line per author ("0000-0000-0000-0000 (A. Ometov)") [S12]. HOWTOSUBMIT does not mention ORCID [S26]. **(inferred)** Include it at camera-ready.
- **Copyright clause** on page 1: the template's "© 2026 Copyright for this paper by its authors. Use permitted under Creative Commons License Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)." [S12] (CEUR model wording in section 5 [S26]).
- **Event footnote** on page 1: must match the proceedings title [S26]. The AITAIS template already contains it [S12].
- **Author agreement:** NTP (Version 1.08-ntp) or TP form, signed, collected by the editors (links in section 5) [S26].
- **Order of sections at the end** [S12]:
  1. Acknowledgements (unnumbered, before references)
  2. Declaration on Generative AI
  3. References
  4. Appendices (after the references, lettered)
  5. Online Resources
