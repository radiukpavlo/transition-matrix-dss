## 2. Topics

Big Data Analytics and Intelligent Systems Applications; Security & Ethics of AI.

The English list on page 3 has 18 topics, numbered here in page order (the same numbering as `03_aitais_venue.md`):

1. Computational Intelligence and Machine Learning Methods

9. Internet of Things, Edge AI, and Digital Twins
10. Big Data Processing, Data Engineering, and Analytics for AI Systems
11. Information Systems, Computer Networks, and Applied AI Platforms
12. Cybersecurity and Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence Systems
13. AI for Information Security, OSINT, and Social Media Intelligence
14. Natural Language Processing and Multilingual Language Technologies
15. Mathematical Modeling, Optimization, and Decision-Support Systems
16. Smart Systems for Enterprises, Cities, and Intelligent Environments
17. Ethical, Legal, and Regulatory Aspects of Artificial Intelligence
18. Emerging Applications and Future Trends in Applied Artificial Intelligence

The Ukrainian list on page 6 is shorter and older (11 topics, e.g. AR/VR in smart systems, smart-network security, computational
intelligence in medicine) and does not match the English list. Use the English list for CMT.

## 3. What this changes for our plan

- **Topic fit.** Topic 7 (AI for Industrial Monitoring and Predictive Maintenance) names our application directly, and topic 8
  (Cyber-Physical Systems) also fits. Decision 17 was therefore revised on 2026-10-22 to primary 7, secondary 1 (it had been
  made from a shortlist of 1, 12 and 15 drawn up before AI4I was chosen); see `06_decisions.md`.
- **Presentation.** The newsletter makes a talk by a co-author at a section session a condition of publication. Our online talk by
  Pavlo Radiuk is agreed with the organising committee; keep their confirmation with the submission records.
- **Review load.** Plan for three reviewers and a second review round between notification (1 Dec) and the event (8-9 Dec).
- **Extended version.** If the paper is accepted, the MECS or Springer extended-version route may become available on the PC's
  recommendation. Any extension must again stay distinct from MAKE.
